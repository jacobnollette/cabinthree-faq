## Wi-Fi and calls

- Network: 🏖️beachhaus - password: "happy place"
- Turn on Wi-Fi calling. The cabin has a metal roof, so cell calls usually struggle indoors. One catch: if you walk away from the cabin during a Wi-Fi call, the call can drop
- The internet is fiber and should be very fast. If you need a wired connection, just ask - Jacob can turn on a port for you
- Please don't stream from illegal sources on our network - no rooted/jailbroken streaming devices, torrenting, or anything similar. The cabin has a static IP address that is literally attached to Jacob - it never changes, so there's zero anonymity here, and anything flagged lands directly on us. We have to be firm on this one

## Smart home

- The cabin lights follow the sun - warmer in the evening, on a circadian rhythm - but every light can also be operated completely manually. The switches always work
- Home Assistant is the optional app we use for smart control of lights, climate, and fun extras like aurora (northern lights) alerts. Curious? Ask us for access - zero pressure

## Audio and video

- Feel free to sign in to your own video and music apps - just leave iCloud (Apple TV and Apple Music) signed in, and leave things how you found them
- If YouTube TV asks to update its location, please say no - it affects our home account
- HomePods are fun! Play to all of them from an iPhone or iPad for whole-cabin, multi-room sound
- The stereo is awesome - crank it up. Just keep the windows closed and aim the sound toward the lake so the neighbors don't hear it. During the day, audio shouldn't be heard within 20 feet of the cabin; at night, it shouldn't be heard outside at all
- One gentle ask: speaker cones are surprisingly delicate, and little fingers love to press them in - please help curious kids admire the speakers from a small distance
- Bringing a gaming console? There's a spare HDMI port, and power is near the desk
- To clean screens or HomePods, use the Woosh bottle from the cleaning supplies with the orange microfiber cloth - spray the cloth, not the screen

## Printing, scanning, and the computer

- One printer (AirPrint, named "Cabin HP") - print as much as you want, it does two-sided. Out of paper? Grab a non-glossy ream at Walmart. Toner low? Let us know and we'll bring more up
- The scanner is free to use - just never run stapled pages through it. You can unplug it from the printer, or use the computer
- There's an iMac at the cabin. Log in with the guest user (password: guest) to print, browse the internet, and so on
