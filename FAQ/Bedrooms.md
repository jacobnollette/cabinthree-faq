## Bedrooms

- Please don't run the Dyson and the hair dryer at the same time - together they can trip a breaker
- The Murphy beds are heavy and can be dangerous - open them slowly, and mind the straps that unlock the bed
- The two beds are different sizes: the bedroom near the living room is a **full-sized** bed, and the main bedroom near the kitchen is a **queen-sized** bed

## Sheets

- There's a spare set of sheets in each bedroom, so you really don't have to wash anything during your stay
- What we'd love: when you leave, strip the used sheets, pop them in the blue bags, and make the beds up fresh with the spare set. We'll wash everything when we come up
- If you'd rather run a load yourself, that's totally fine - just please wash the sheets on their own. Mixed in with other laundry they can snag or get damaged, and we'd hate to lose them
- Match the size to the bed - full sheets on the full bed, queen sheets on the queen. The full bedroom's spare sheets are on the upper closet shelf in that room; the main (queen) bedroom's spare sheets are on the shelf above its closet

## Towels

- Beach towels and extra towels are in both bedrooms - mostly in the secondary bedroom (the full bed near the living room). Extra hand towels are in the bathroom
- Used towels can go in the blue bags too - we'll take care of them
- Please keep wet towels off the wood floors - hang them up, or toss them in the shower to dry
